# The Culture of ReplicaFlow

> **Great communities are built on respect, curiosity and collaboration.**

ReplicaFlow is more than software.

It is a community of people who share a common goal:

> **Make Information Flow understandable.**

This document does not exist to list rules.

It exists to describe the culture we want to build together.

Culture is not enforced by rules alone.

It is created by the everyday actions of every member of the community.

---

# Our Philosophy

We believe that great software is built by communities, not individuals.

Every contributor brings unique experiences, ideas and perspectives.

Some have decades of experience.

Some are writing their very first Pull Request.

Both are valuable.

> **Experience deserves respect. Fresh perspectives deserve attention.**

---

# Respect

Treat every member of the community with respect.

Behind every username is a person.

Disagreement is natural.

Disrespect is not.

---

# Challenge Ideas, Not People

Healthy discussions improve projects.

Personal attacks destroy them.

Challenge assumptions.

Question designs.

Debate technical decisions.

But never attack the person behind an idea.

A good discussion is one where everyone leaves with a better understanding, regardless of the outcome.

---

# Learning Together

ReplicaFlow is a place to learn.

No one knows everything.

No one is expected to know everything.

Questions are welcome.

Ideas are welcome.

Different opinions are welcome.

> **Every contributor was once a beginner.**

---

# Mistakes Are Part of Learning

Making mistakes is not something to fear.

It is one of the most valuable ways to learn.

Innovation requires experimentation.

Growth requires curiosity.

A mistake is not a failure.

Ignoring what it teaches us is.

> **Making mistakes is part of learning.**

---

# We Explain

ReplicaFlow values understanding over simply being right.

When reviewing contributions, explaining *why* is often more valuable than simply saying *no*.

We believe that knowledge grows when it is shared.

Help others understand.

Teach whenever possible.

Explain your reasoning.

---

# We Listen

Every opinion deserves to be heard.

Not every opinion will change ReplicaFlow.

But every respectful discussion helps us understand different perspectives.

Listening comes before deciding.

---

# Criticism

Criticism is welcome.

Constructive criticism is invaluable.

If you think ReplicaFlow can be improved, tell us.

Help us understand your perspective.

Explain the problem.

Suggest a better solution.

Good criticism helps great projects become even better.

---

# Community Over Ego

ReplicaFlow does not exist to prove who is right.

It exists to solve problems together.

Good ideas are evaluated by their value, not by the person who proposed them.

The best solution can come from anyone.

---

# Good Intent

Assume good intent.

Most contributors are here because they want to help.

Before assuming bad intentions, ask questions.

Seek understanding.

Give people the opportunity to explain.

Kindness creates better communities.

---

# Creating a Safe Community

To keep ReplicaFlow welcoming for everyone, we do not accept:

- personal attacks
- harassment
- discrimination
- hate speech
- intimidation
- deliberate disruption of discussions
- abusive or offensive language

These behaviors are incompatible with the culture we want to build.

---

# Maintaining the Culture

Core Maintainers are responsible for protecting the culture of ReplicaFlow.

When necessary, they may moderate discussions, request changes, remove inappropriate content or restrict participation.

These decisions should always be guided by the project's philosophy and the long-term health of the community.

The goal is never punishment.

The goal is to preserve a respectful, collaborative environment where everyone can contribute.

---

# Growing Together

ReplicaFlow will continue to evolve.

Its software will grow.

Its documentation will grow.

Its community will grow.

Every respectful discussion.

Every thoughtful question.

Every honest review.

Every contribution.

They all help shape the future of ReplicaFlow.

Together, we build something that none of us could build alone.

---

# Thank You

Thank you for helping create the kind of community you would like to be part of.

The culture of ReplicaFlow will never be defined by this document alone.

It will be defined by the people who choose to live it every day.

> **See your data move.**
